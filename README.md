# RappterBox Experiments

Public RAPP Vision channel for evidence-backed RappterBox experiments.

Every episode begins with a bounded prompt, shows the visible result, and ends
with source-linked evidence. Synthetic worlds and inhabitants are labeled as
such; hardware, executions, captures, and evidence records are not presented
as customer outcomes.

Watch through the canonical player:

https://kody-w.github.io/rapp-vision/#/channel/rappterbox

## Genesis 251 Starting Houses Draft

The live replay at `draft/index.html` reconstructs the inaugural paper-card
draft from 254 public RAPP/1 frames: one opening, one starter declaration, 251
named picks, and one close.

Every player joins exactly one of four starting houses. House membership is
stored only in that browser as one lowercase house code; the replay collects no
player name, email, account, device identifier, or network record. Houses add
identity and community, not gameplay power, companion access, draft odds, or
economic weight.

The current public founder profiles represent the houses:

| Card | House | Founder profile | First unique starter |
|---|---|---|---|

| 002 | Scout | Sawyer | Everframe-001 |

This is a watch-only prerelease simulation. It issues no metal card, title,
purchase, descendant, or ownership right. Any later product state requires a
separate rights-cleared, signed release; this replay grants nothing.
